# Exp 8: Reproducing an Image Using Prompts for Image Generation

### Date : 07/09/2026
### Reg. No. : 212223230176

## Aim:
To demonstrate the ability of text-to-image generation tools to reproduce an existing image by crafting precise prompts. The goal is to identify key elements within the image and use these details to generate an image as close as possible to the original.

## Tools Used
* ChatGPT Image Generation (DALL·E)
* Text-to-Image Prompting

## Image 1: A Japanese garden with flowers.






## 1. Analysis of the Given Image
The image represents a peaceful alpine landscape during sunset.

Objects/Subjects: Mountains, lake, trees, rocks, flowers and sunset
Colors: Orange, golden yellow, purple, blue and pink
Lighting: Warm golden-hour sunlight
Background: Snow-covered mountains and colorful sky
Texture: Natural rocks, grass, flowers and calm water
Composition: Mountains on the left, lake in the center and sunset on the right
Style: Realistic cinematic landscape photography
Mood: Peaceful, beautiful and majestic

## 2. Basic Prompt
A Japanese garden with flowers.


## 3. Refined Prompt

A peaceful Japanese garden filled with colorful flowers, surrounded by lush green plants, traditional elements, a calm pond, rocks and a small wooden bridge. Soft natural light creates a relaxing and harmonious atmosphere.

## 4. Final Prompt

A cinematic and serene Japanese garden in spring, featuring a colorful flower-filled meadow with cherry blossoms, pink and white flowers, lush green shrubs and carefully arranged rocks in the foreground. A calm koi pond reflects the surrounding trees and sky, with a traditional wooden bridge and elegant Japanese stone lanterns adding cultural detail. Soft golden sunlight filters through the trees, creating gentle highlights and shadows. Realistic photography, natural textures, vibrant yet harmonious colors, detailed foliage, balanced composition, soft depth of field, high detail and a peaceful Zen atmosphere.

## 5. Generated Image

Title: Serene Japanese Flower Garden

The final image was generated using the refined prompt. It contains the major elements associated with a Japanese garden: colorful flowers, cherry blossoms, green plants, rocks, a calm pond, a wooden bridge, stone lanterns and soft natural lighting, creating a peaceful and harmonious atmosphere.

## 6. Observation

The generated image successfully reproduced the main characteristics of the Japanese garden concept. The arrangement of colorful flowers, greenery, rocks, pond and traditional garden elements was closely represented. Additional details such as Japanese lanterns, wooden structures and natural textures improved the realism and beauty of the generated image.


## Image 2: A cup of coffee in a café.

## 1. Analysis of the Given Image

The image represents a warm and cozy café setting featuring a freshly prepared cup of coffee.

Objects/Subjects: Cup of coffee, saucer, wooden table, café furniture, chairs, tables, plants and decorative elements
Colors: Brown, cream, beige, white, dark green and warm orange
Lighting: Soft warm café lighting with natural sunlight entering through the windows
Background: Cozy café interior with tables, chairs, plants, shelves and subtle decorative items
Texture: Smooth ceramic cup, wooden table surface, creamy coffee foam, fabric chairs and natural plant textures
Composition: The coffee cup is placed prominently in the foreground on a table, while the café interior appears softly blurred in the background
Style: Realistic, cinematic café photography
Mood: Warm, relaxing, peaceful and inviting 

## 2. Basic Prompt

A cup of coffee on a table in a cozy café.

## 3. Refined Prompt

A freshly brewed cup of coffee on a wooden table inside a cozy café, surrounded by warm lighting, comfortable furniture, plants and soft background details.

## 4. Final Prompt

A cinematic and highly detailed photograph of a freshly brewed cup of coffee placed on a wooden table inside a cozy modern café. The coffee is served in a simple elegant ceramic cup with a creamy layer of foam on top, accompanied by a small saucer and spoon. Warm golden sunlight enters through large café windows, creating soft highlights and gentle shadows across the table. The background contains comfortable chairs, wooden tables, green plants, shelves and subtle decorative elements, softly blurred with a shallow depth of field. Natural wood textures, realistic coffee foam, warm brown and beige tones, soft bokeh, realistic lighting and a peaceful inviting atmosphere, captured in realistic cinematic café photography style.

## 5. Generated Image

Title: A Cup of Coffee in a Cozy Café

The final image was generated using the refined prompt. It includes the main characteristics of the café concept such as a freshly brewed cup of coffee, wooden table, warm lighting, café furniture, plants and a softly blurred background.

## 6. Observation

The generated image successfully reproduced the main characteristics of the cozy café concept. The coffee cup, wooden table, warm lighting and café interior were closely represented. Additional details such as coffee foam, saucer, spoon, plants and soft background blur improved the realism and created a warm and relaxing café atmosphere.

## Conclusion:
By using detailed and well-crafted prompts, text-to-image generation models can be effective in reproducing an image closely. The quality of the generated image depends on how accurately the prompt describes the image's key elements. The experiment demonstrates the importance of prompt refinement and iteration when working with AI tools to achieve desired outcomes. With practice, the model can generate images that closely match real-world visuals, which is useful for creative and practical applications.
